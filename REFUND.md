# Refund Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-13_

Korokokok v0.9.2 is available as a paid **Early Access** release ($69 perpetual license). The policy below applies to all purchases.

## Try the free trial first

Every install includes a **14-day free trial** with all features unlocked — no card required up front. Use the trial to confirm Korokokok works on your hardware and fits your workflow before you buy.

## 14-day refund window

If you purchase a license and are not satisfied, you may request a refund within **14 days of purchase**.

### When refunds apply

Refunds will be granted for:

- The software not working on your system despite meeting the stated requirements
- Critical bugs that prevent you from completing core tasks
- Purchase made by mistake (duplicate order, wrong product)

### When refunds do not apply

Refunds will not be available for:

- "Changed my mind" requests after 14 days
- Hardware that does not meet the published minimum requirements (see [README](README.md#system-requirements))
- Inability to run the software due to modifications, incompatible third-party software, or user error
- AI-generated outputs not matching your expectations (results vary by prompt, hardware, and model)

## How to request a refund

Refund requests are handled through the payment provider (Polar). Reply to your Polar receipt email — or email [korokokok.storystudio@gmail.com](mailto:korokokok.storystudio@gmail.com) — with a brief reason and confirmation that you want the license deactivated. Refunds are processed within 5 business days.

## Questions before buying

Not sure if Korokokok will work for you? Install the **14-day free trial** first — zero risk, no card, every feature unlocked. If you still have questions, email [korokokok.storystudio@gmail.com](mailto:korokokok.storystudio@gmail.com) and I'll answer honestly before you commit.
